# Engineering Constitution v1.0

1. Specification precedes implementation.
2. Requirements, scenarios, tasks, code, tests and evidence must be traceable.
3. Orchestration must be stateful, non-linear, dependency-aware and persisted.
4. Humans own material ambiguity, architecture, policy exceptions, security-sensitive decisions, destructive actions, release readiness and final quality.
5. Retry is bounded; unsafe continuation enters safe-stop.
6. Rollback is distinguished from compensation; no false reversibility claims.
7. Policy is versioned and release-blocking when mandatory checks fail.
8. Audit evidence must reconstruct workflow execution.
9. Demonstration measurements are labeled and are not presented as production statistics.
10. AI output is proposal/execution assistance; it is not human approval.
