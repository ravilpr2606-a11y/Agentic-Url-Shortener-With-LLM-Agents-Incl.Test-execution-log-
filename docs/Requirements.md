# Requirements

I keep the authoritative requirements in `specs/001-agentic-url-shortener/spec.md`.

I used the assignment and execution guide to drive the requirement model: requirement understanding, decomposition, brownfield reasoning, non-linear stateful orchestration, human governance, reliability/recovery, policy guardrails, observability/auditability, traceability and three materially distinct scenarios.

I keep the URL shortener as the demonstration domain and distinguish confirmed requirements, assumptions, exclusions and evidence limitations rather than silently filling gaps.
